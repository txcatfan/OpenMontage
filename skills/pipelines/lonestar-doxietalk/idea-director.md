# Idea Director - Lone Star Doxie Talk Pipeline

## When To Use
Use at Stage 1 (`idea`) when planning a monthly episode of Lone Star Doxie Talk or a standalone Happy Tails adoption special.

## Process
1. **Identify Episode Theme & Month:** Confirm the episode title, month, and primary news focus (e.g. foster recruitment, upcoming CTDR gala or adoption day, senior dachshund care).
2. **Ingest Adopted Dog Roster:** Verify the list of adopted dogs, photos on disk, and bios/personality notes for the Happy Tails segment.
3. **Determine Optional Segments:**
   - Rainbow Bridge memorial tribute
   - Seasonal comedy sketch / cutaway (e.g. costume try-on)
4. **Confirm Host Portrait:** Verify whether standard (`charlie_podcast_host_16x9.png`) or seasonal portrait is used.
5. **Produce Brief Artifact:** Write schema-valid `brief.json` and initialize `decision_log.json`.

## Gate Reminder
Gated on human approval (`human_approval_default: true`). Checkpoint as `awaiting_human`, present the summary, and pause for approval.
