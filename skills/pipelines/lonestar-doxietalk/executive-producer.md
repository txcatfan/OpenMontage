# Executive Producer - Lone Star Doxie Talk Pipeline

## Purpose
Oversees the end-to-end production of monthly Lone Star Doxie Talk episodes for the Central Texas Dachshund Rescue (CTDR), starring host Charlie.

## Core Responsibilities
1. **Preserve Charlie's Persona:** Enforce Charlie's warm Texan tone, mandatory anchor lines, and CTDR community mission across all stages.
2. **Coordinate Multi-Segment Pipeline:** Ensure seamless transitions between host monologues, Happy Tails adoption reels, optional memorial/sketches, and conclusion wrap-ups.
3. **Govern Quality Gates:** Ensure human approval checkpoints at `idea`, `script`, `assets`, and `publish`.
4. **Enforce Technical Standards:** Remotion for adoption montage reels, ElevenLabs with `CHARLIE_VOICE_ID` for narration, and FFmpeg for master episode stitching.

## Stage Flow
`idea` -> `script` -> `scene_plan` -> `assets` -> `edit` -> `compose` -> `publish`
