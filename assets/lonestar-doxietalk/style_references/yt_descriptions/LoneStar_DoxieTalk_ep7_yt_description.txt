Lone Star Doxie Talk Episode 7: Buda Success & Mother's Day Tribute 🐾🏁
Welcome back to Lone Star Doxie Talk! In today's episode, we're recapping a massive weekend at the 2026 Buda Wiener Dog Races, taking a moment to celebrate the moms who hold our packs together, and meeting our newest CTDR graduates.

🏆 Buda Wiener Dog Races Recap
A huge thank you to the trusty band of volunteers who made our best-run CTDR booth ever possible! We are breaking down the highlights:

The Racing: A big shoutout to CTDR alum Astro for making the quarterfinals in his debut, and to our legend Jimmy Dean for a spectacular final race before his retirement!

Our Mission: We raised several thousand dollars to help with extra vet and boarding costs, and educated future adopters on the long-and-low way of life.

Save the Date: Join us at our next big event, Wienerfest in the Austin area on October 31st!

❤️ Mother's Day Tribute
In honor of the upcoming holiday, Charlie takes a moment to thank all the amazing dog moms and leaders of the pack out there who keep our rescues happy, healthy, and loved. We appreciate you more than a sprinkle of cheese on top of our supper!
