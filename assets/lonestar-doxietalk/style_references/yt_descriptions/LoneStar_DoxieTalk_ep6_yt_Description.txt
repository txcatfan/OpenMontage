Lone Star Doxie Talk Episode 6: Racing to Adoption! 🐾🏁
Welcome back to Lone Star Doxie Talk! In today’s episode, we’re shifting into high gear as we prepare for the most anticipated event of the year—the 2026 Buda Wiener Dog Races!

🏎️ Oakley’s Training Montage
The competition is fierce, but nobody is working harder than Oakley! We take you behind the scenes of her intense training regime. Will all that dedication pay off on the track? We’re wishing her the best of luck as she represents the long-and-low community!

🎪 Visit Central Texas Dachshund Rescue (CTDR) at the Buda Wiener Dog Races
Headed to the races? Make sure to stop by the Central Texas Dachshund Rescue booth! We are proud to be the only dachshund rescue represented at the event this year.

Location: Right next to the race track and the grandstand.

Why Visit: Meet our volunteers, learn about our mission, and see photos of all the precious doxies currently waiting for their forever homes.

🏡 March 2026 Adoption Success Stories
It’s time for "Happy Tails!" Thanks to your incredible support, March was a record-breaking month. Join Charlie as he presents the 8 rescued dogs who found their forever families this month. From "foster fails" to boarding-to-home miracles, these stories are why we do what we do.
