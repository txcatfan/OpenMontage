Lone Star Doxie Talk Episode 8: NBA Finals & Summer Rescue Roundup! 🐾🏀
Mosey on in and sit a spell with us for Episode 8 of Lone Star Doxie Talk! Today, Charlie is talking basketball grit, crucial foster needs, a major mid-year fundraising push, and some sweet summer savings to help our long-and-low friends find their forever homesteads.

🏀 Go Spurs Go!
Before getting down to rescue matters, we are sending some huge Texas-sized congratulations to our San Antonio Spurs on making it to the NBA Finals! Built on pure grit and determination, the whole Texas low-rider community is barking loud and rooting for you against the Knicks!

🏡 An Urgent Call for Foster Heroes
CTDR has welcomed a wagon-load of 13 new dachshunds in recent weeks, many from shelters where time was running out. While we are incredibly grateful to Dr. Driscoll at Day Lily Animal Hospital in Hutto for giving them a temporary bunk, boarding costs are stacking up fast. Fostering is how you can ride in and save the day!

Become a Foster: Learn how you can fill your heart to the brim at:  https://www.ctdr.org/info/display?PageID=12474

💰 The $11,000 Mid-Year Fundraiser
All those new arrivals mean a lot of vet care—from initial shots and spay/neuter surgeries to extensive heartworm treatments and healing injuries. We are trying to rustle up $11,000 to cover these essential needs. We’ve rounded up almost $2,200 so far, but we need your help to reach the finish line! Every single dollar helps mend a broken heart.
